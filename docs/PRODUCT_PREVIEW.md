# ComfyUI-MiniMaxH3-Context — Product Preview

## Turn Ideas, References, and Creative Direction into Generation-Ready Video Context

**Coming soon — open source.**

ComfyUI-MiniMaxH3-Context is a creator-focused toolkit that transforms your ideas into clear, structured, generation-ready context for MiniMax H3 video workflows.

Describe the scene you want, add optional images or reference media, and let the workflow organize the subjects, actions, camera direction, timing, dialogue, visual style, and audio intent. The result is a polished prompt ready to connect directly to your video generation workflow.

No complicated prompt engineering. No hidden processing. No repetitive copying and pasting.

## From Creative Intent to a Complete Video Plan

Start with a simple idea and build it into a coherent video direction.

ComfyUI-MiniMaxH3-Context helps organize:

- Subjects and character details
- Scenes and environments
- Actions and movement
- Camera framing and motion
- Visual style and atmosphere
- Timeline and shot progression
- Dialogue and spoken content
- Captions, signs, and visible text
- Music, ambience, and sound direction
- Image and video references
- First-frame and last-frame guidance
- Important creative constraints

Your original instructions remain at the center of the process. Exact dialogue, lyrics, captions, signs, and other important details are preserved rather than silently rewritten.

## One Sidebar, Every Creative Mode

The built-in Sidebar App Mode provides a simple, guided experience directly inside ComfyUI.

The first release is designed to support:

- **Text to Video** — Create a complete video direction from a written idea.
- **Image to Video** — Use an opening image as the visual starting point.
- **First and Last Frame to Video** — Guide the transformation between two key images.
- **Last Frame to Video** — Build a sequence toward a defined final image.
- **Reference to Video** — Use ordered image and video references to guide characters, environments, motion, style, and continuity.

Each mode presents only the controls that matter for that workflow, keeping the experience focused and approachable.

## Open, Inspectable, and ComfyUI-Native

The experience is streamlined without hiding what happens behind the scenes.

The sidebar can prepare and connect a visible ComfyUI workflow for you. You can inspect the nodes, review the prompt, adjust the workflow, and decide exactly how generation should proceed.

Your final prompt can flow directly into a compatible downstream generation node, so copying and pasting is optional—not required.

Use it through:

- The guided Sidebar App Mode
- Individual ComfyUI nodes
- Reusable subgraphs
- Ready-made workflows
- Your own custom ComfyUI pipelines

## Understand Before You Generate

A great video prompt should be more than a block of text.

Alongside the final prompt, ComfyUI-MiniMaxH3-Context provides an organized report showing how your request was understood, including:

- The selected generation mode
- The intended duration
- Reference roles and ordering
- Important creative requirements
- Scene and timeline structure
- Missing or uncertain information
- Compatibility notes
- Validation results
- Clear limitations before generation begins

This makes it easier to review your creative direction, catch mistakes early, and understand exactly what will be sent downstream.

## Built for Reference-Heavy Workflows

Working with multiple references can quickly become confusing. ComfyUI-MiniMaxH3-Context keeps each asset identifiable and assigns it a clear purpose.

References can be organized by roles such as:

- Character or subject
- Environment or scene
- Visual style
- First frame
- Last frame
- Motion reference
- Audio reference
- Continuity reference

Reference order remains stable throughout the workflow, helping prevent incorrect image assignments and unexpected results.

## A Smoother Creative Workflow

The Sidebar App Mode is designed to feel ready from the moment it opens.

You can:

1. Choose a creation mode.
2. Describe your idea.
3. Add the required images or references.
4. Review the interpreted context.
5. Start the connected ComfyUI workflow.
6. Inspect the final prompt and report.
7. Continue directly into video generation.

If an existing canvas cannot be used safely, the sidebar asks what you want to do before making changes. Your workflow is never silently replaced.

## Local-First and Privacy-Conscious

Your creative material stays under your control.

ComfyUI-MiniMaxH3-Context does not silently choose an online provider, upload media, or hide network activity behind a convenient button. Remote services remain optional and require an explicit choice.

The project does not bundle model weights. Video generation remains handled by the compatible model and generation nodes you choose.

## Designed for Creators and Workflow Builders

Whether you prefer a guided interface or detailed node-level control, the same creative workflow remains available.

- Creators get a clear path from idea to generation.
- Prompt designers get structured, reviewable outputs.
- Workflow builders get reusable components.
- Advanced users retain full control over the visible graph.
- Teams get more consistent prompts and reference handling.

## Independent and Open Source

ComfyUI-MiniMaxH3-Context is an independent project created for the ComfyUI community. It is not an official MiniMax implementation and does not claim to reproduce private MiniMax systems.

Its goal is simple:

> Make complex H3 context preparation easier to use, easier to inspect, and easier to connect to real ComfyUI generation workflows.
